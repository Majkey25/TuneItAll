# Privacy

TuneItAll works offline and requests microphone access only while the tuner is
active. Audio samples are analyzed transiently in device memory and discarded
immediately. They are not recorded, retained, shared, or transmitted.

The app has no account system, ads, analytics, tracking, telemetry, remote
crash reporting, or Internet permission. Preferences, favorites, and custom
tunings stay in Android application storage on the device.

The complete English and Czech policies are in [`docs/privacy`](docs/privacy).
Recheck this statement and the Google Play Data Safety form before every
release that changes permissions or dependencies.
