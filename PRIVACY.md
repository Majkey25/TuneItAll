# Privacy

Intoniva works offline. Microphone samples are analyzed transiently while the
tuner is active, then discarded. User-selected song files are decoded only in
memory for chord, note, or tempo analysis. Audio is not uploaded, retained,
shared, or transmitted.

The app has no account system, ads, analytics, tracking, telemetry, remote
crash reporting, or Internet permission. Preferences, favorites, custom
tunings, and trainer scores stay in Android application storage on the device.
Optional Auto-scroll uses a gesture-only Accessibility service after an
explicit disclosure and does not read screen content.

The complete English and Czech policies are in [`docs/privacy`](docs/privacy).
Recheck this statement and the Google Play Data Safety form before every
release that changes permissions or dependencies.
