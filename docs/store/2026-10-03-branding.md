# October 2026 branding update

Version: 0.3.0-alpha.35 (38). Package: `com.tuneitall.tuner`.

## Icon

The publisher supplied the ivory tuning fork design. ImageGen regenerated a
full-square master and a dark-green background. Two transparent cutouts had
ragged edges and were rejected. The shipped fork is a clean native vector
following the generated silhouette, on the ImageGen background.

[Source notes and prompts](../../assets/source/ICON.md) record the asset origins.
`tools/RenderStoreIcon.java` exports all icon variants from the same geometry.
The 49dp mark is centered in the 108dp adaptive layers. The Play icon uses the
same visible crop as the launcher. Notifications use a separate, larger mark.

## Device checks

Huawei YAL-L21, Android 10, QA package `com.tuneitall.tuner.qa`:

- Both launcher icons rendered centered at the expected scale.
- Monochrome and notification marks passed native bounds checks.
- `BrandIconTest` and `AutoScrollManifestTest`: four tests passed.
- The QA icon was inspected in the Huawei app drawer and on an actual Home page.
- Opening the Home shortcut launched the QA package, not the old production app.
- The temporary shortcut was removed and the original Home layout restored.
- No production app data or audio settings changed. No audio played through the PC.

The Home screenshot stays local because it includes personal wallpaper.
The production installation keeps its old icon until the store update arrives.

## Store captures

`StoreScreenshotTest` captured eight English and eight Czech screens on the
Huawei. Sources are in `source-captures/2026-10-03/` (1080×2232 app content).
The capture test is opt-in through the `captureStore=true` instrumentation argument.

These captures use production composables with reproducible fixture state.
The tuner fixture passes a synthetic signal through YIN and `TunerEngine`.
The song fixture passes a synthetic C–Am–F–G progression through the real chord
analyzer. They demonstrate the interface, not real-instrument accuracy.
They contain no private songs, recordings, or microphone input.

`tools/RenderStoreScreenshots.java` frames the captures at 1080×1920 and exports
1024×500 feature graphics for both languages. Both contact sheets were inspected
for cropping, readable controls, and consistent new branding.

## Local verification

Passed. JVM results: 345 tests, zero failures, errors, or skips. The opt-in

dependencies, or the application ID.
