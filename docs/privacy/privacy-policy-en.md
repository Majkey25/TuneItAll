# Intoniva Privacy Policy

Effective date: 4 September 2026
Last updated: 4 September 2026

Intoniva is published by MajkeyLab. Privacy contact:
`majkeylab@gmail.com`.

Intoniva is an offline Android music tool. It has no accounts, advertising,
analytics, profiling, tracking, maintainer-operated server, or Android Internet
permission. MajkeyLab does not collect, sell, or share data through the app.

## On-device processing

Intoniva uses microphone access while the tuner is active. Samples are processed
transiently in memory to estimate pitch and are not recorded, retained, uploaded,
or shared.

When you select a song through Android's document picker, Intoniva can read that
file to estimate chords, notes, tempo, and an acoustic arrangement. The source
file is not copied or uploaded. Analysis results remain in memory and disappear
when they are replaced or the app process ends.

Settings, favorites, custom tunings, metronome settings, Auto-scroll acceptance,
and trainer scores remain in the app's private local storage. You can delete them
by clearing app data or uninstalling Intoniva.

Auto-scroll is optional. If enabled in Android Accessibility settings, it can
perform deterministic swipe gestures initiated through the floating controls.
Its configuration disables window-content retrieval. It does not read, inspect,
record, store, or transmit screen content. Closing the controls stops scrolling.

For this local processing, MajkeyLab receives no personal data. Where a legal
basis is required, processing is necessary to provide the feature you request
and reflects MajkeyLab's legitimate interest in providing an offline music tool.
There are no app recipients or international transfers.

## External links and optional support

The support button asks Android to open Buy Me a Coffee in an external browser.
The app sends no identifier and unlocks no feature. The browser and destination
apply their own privacy terms.

If you voluntarily email MajkeyLab or open a GitHub issue, MajkeyLab and the
email or GitHub provider may receive the address or account name, message, and
attachments you choose to send. Do not attach recordings or sensitive data.
MajkeyLab uses correspondence only to provide requested support, prevent abuse,
or meet legal obligations. The legal basis is steps requested by you or
MajkeyLab's legitimate interests. Correspondence is normally deleted within 12
months after the request closes, unless you request earlier deletion or a legal
duty requires longer retention. Provider processing and international transfers
are governed by that provider's terms and safeguards.

## European Union and EEA rights

Subject to applicable law, you may request access, correction, deletion,
restriction, portability, or object to processing by contacting
`majkeylab@gmail.com`. You may also complain to the data-protection authority
for your residence, work, or the alleged infringement. MajkeyLab has no
server-side app record to access, correct, export, or delete; requests can apply
to optional support correspondence.

## United States privacy rights

Intoniva does not sell or share personal information, use targeted advertising,
offer a financial incentive, or discriminate after a privacy request. Optional
support identifiers and correspondence are the only personal information
MajkeyLab may receive. Contact `majkeylab@gmail.com` to exercise rights that
apply in your state.

## Children

Intoniva is a general-audience music tool and is not directed to children. The
app does not collect personal information. A child must not send personal
information through an optional support channel without a parent or guardian.

## Changes

MajkeyLab will update this policy before distributing a version that materially
changes data handling.
