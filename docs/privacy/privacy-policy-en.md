# TuneItAll Privacy Policy

Effective date: 9 August 2026

TuneItAll is an offline instrument tuner. It does not collect, retain, sell,
share, or transmit personal data.

## Microphone

TuneItAll requests microphone permission to detect the pitch of an instrument.
Audio samples are processed transiently in memory on the device while the tuner
is active. They are not recorded, written to storage, retained, transmitted, or
shared. Capture stops when the active tuner leaves the foreground.

## Network and third parties

The app does not request Android internet permission. It contains no account,
advertising, analytics, tracking, crash-reporting, or cloud service integration.

## Stored preferences

Mode, selected tuning, A4 reference, notation, headstock layout, favorites, and
custom tunings are stored locally on the device. Uninstalling the app removes
this data, subject to the device's Android backup settings.

## Children and changes

TuneItAll does not knowingly collect data from anyone, including children.
This policy must be updated before any release that changes data handling or
adds a network-capable dependency.

Contact details and a public policy URL must be supplied by the publisher in
Google Play Console before publication.
