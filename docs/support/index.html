Support

Get Intoniva working.

The tuner cannot hear the instrument

  • Grant microphone permission.
  • For an unplugged electric guitar, open Settings → Tuner and choose Unplugged electric.
  • Keep microphone sensitivity high and adjust needle stability separately if needed.

The metronome is silent

  • Raise the Android media volume.
  • Open Settings → Metronome and confirm that Mute is off.
  • Raise the metronome volume and try another sound style.

Change the app language

Open Settings → General → App language. Choose the system default, English, Czech, German, French, or Spanish.

Optional support

Intoniva is free. The optional Buy Me a Coffee page helps fund maintenance. A tip does not unlock features or change support priority.

Report a problem

Email majkeylab@gmail.com for private support or use public GitHub Issues. Include the app version, Android version, device model, and steps needed to reproduce the problem. Do not attach private recordings, payment details, or sensitive information. The privacy policy describes optional support correspondence.

Podpora v češtině

Pokud ladička neslyší nástroj, povolte mikrofon a v Nastavení → Ladička vyberte profil pro tichou místnost. Pokud neslyšíte metronom, zvyšte systémovou hlasitost médií a zkontrolujte, že metronom není ztlumený.

Soukromé dotazy: majkeylab@gmail.com. GitHub issues jsou veřejné. Nezveřejňujte soukromé nahrávky ani platební a citlivé údaje.
