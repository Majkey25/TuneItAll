Support

Get TuneItAll working.

The tuner cannot hear the instrument

  • Grant microphone permission.
  • Open Settings → Tuner and choose Quiet room.
  • Move the instrument closer to the microphone and reduce background noise.

The metronome is silent

  • Raise the Android media volume.
  • Open Settings → Metronome and confirm that Mute is off.
  • Raise the metronome volume and try another sound style.

Report a problem

Open a report in GitHub Issues. Include the TuneItAll version, Android version, device model, and exact steps. Do not attach private recordings or personal information.

Podpora v češtině

Pokud ladička neslyší nástroj, povolte mikrofon a v Nastavení → Ladička vyberte profil pro tichou místnost. Pokud neslyšíte metronom, zvyšte systémovou hlasitost médií a zkontrolujte, že metronom není ztlumený.
