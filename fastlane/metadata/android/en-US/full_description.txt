TuneItAll opens straight into accurate, distraction-free tuning.

Tune guitar, bass, or ukulele automatically, select a string manually, or use the chromatic tuner for any pitched instrument. The clear cents rail shows whether the note is flat, sharp, or in tune.

Features:
• Guitar support for 6, 7, 8, and 9 strings
• Licensed 3+3 and 6-inline guitar headstock artwork, plus 7 to 9 strings
• Four-string bass and ukulele
• Standard, lowered, drop, open, and DADGAD presets
• Search, favorites, and custom tunings
• Adjustable A4 reference from 410.0 to 480.0 Hz
• Sharps or flats note naming
• Click-free reference tone when a string is tapped
• Stable in-tune confirmation sound
• Adjustable tuner profiles, microphone sensitivity, response, stability, noise rejection, harmonic protection, tolerance, timing, and audio input
• Mechanical metronome from 20 to 400 BPM with meter, subdivision, accent, sound, volume, mute, and count-in controls
• Canonical Standard E guitar and Standard C ukulele chord diagrams
• Experimental offline chord timeline for a user-selected local audio file, with playback and transposition
• Chord learning, hidden-answer chord quiz, and 12-note ear training with local scoring
• Simple Light and Dark interface

TuneItAll has no ads, account, analytics, tracking, or internet permission. Microphone audio is analyzed transiently on-device and never recorded, retained, shared, or transmitted.

Selected song files are decoded only on-device and are never copied or uploaded.
